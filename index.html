Strength

Prestiging banks one prestige and restarts the XP cycle at Level 1. Coins, gear, history, and double-XP charges all carry over.

2× XP

Claiming only banks a charge. Activating switches one banked charge on — only the next session logged while it is on pays double, then it switches off. A session started before activation is never doubled, and no button can change an already-saved session.

How lifts score

Move-up (more weight, or more total reps at the same weight): +1 XP. Goal/milestone hit (the goal checkbox on a lift): +1 XP, even on a held or reset lift. Held or reset without a goal hit: +0 XP. First-time lift: +1 XP. Doubled sessions pay twice. These rules apply to sessions logged from now on — seeded and synced history is never rescored.

Strength Board

Progress = move-ups, Held = same weight and reps, Reset = fewer reps or less weight. First-time lifts (no previous session to compare) aren't counted.
