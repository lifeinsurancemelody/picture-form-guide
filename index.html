Claiming only banks a charge. Activating switches one banked charge on — only the next session logged while it is on pays double, then it switches off. A session started before activation is never doubled, and no button can change an already-saved session.

How lifts score

Move-up (more weight, or more total reps at the same weight): +2 XP. Held (same weight, same total reps): +1 XP. Reset (fewer reps, or less weight): +0 XP. Doubled sessions pay twice.

Bodyweight

+2 XP per week of average gain: when a new week's average weigh-in beats the most recent earlier week with weigh-ins, and that week hasn't paid out yet.

Workout history

Every session, lift by lift. Deleting a lift or a session recalculates XP on the spot. Seeded history from before this app predates its XP ledger, so deleting a seeded lift does not change the XP total.
